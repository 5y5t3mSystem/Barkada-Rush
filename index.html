HOW TO PLAY

Barkada Rush is a multiplayer party game made for friends, classmates, and groups — perfect for game nights, hangouts, and barkada gatherings.

01 — CREATE OR JOIN A LOBBY

Players can create a game room or join an existing one. Future versions will use a room code or QR code to make joining easier.

02 — GET YOUR BARKADA TOGETHER

Once everyone joins the same room, everyone can play together — the game stays synced for all players.

03 — ANSWER AND VOTE

The game presents questions, polls, and fun challenges. Players answer or vote depending on the round.

04 — WHO GETS THE MOST VOTES?

The player with the most votes becomes the selected player for the challenge.

05 — PICK 1 OF 5 CARDS

The selected player picks one of five cards. Each card has a different challenge or activity to do.

06 — HAVE FUN WITH YOUR BARKADA

Keep playing through rounds of questions, votes, and challenges with your barkada!
